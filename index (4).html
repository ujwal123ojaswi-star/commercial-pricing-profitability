How it is calculated

Pocket priceList price less on-invoice discount gives invoice price. Invoice price less volume rebates, freight per ton, and payment-terms cost (invoice revenue × terms days / 365 × cost of capital) gives pocket price.

Price realizationFY2026 tons valued at FY2026 versus FY2025 per-product prices, so a shift in product mix does not inflate or hide the price change. Realization rate is the realized pocket price change divided by the announced increase.

Price-volume-mixVolume: change in total tons at the FY2025 average price. Mix: FY2026 tons at each product's FY2025 price, less FY2026 tons at the average price. Price: FY2026 tons times each product's price change.

Customer profitabilityPocket revenue less standard cost of goods gives pocket margin; subtracting annual cost-to-serve (technical service, sales coverage) gives net profit.

Renewal upliftFor a customer below target margin t, the price increase needed with cost and volume held constant is (t × R − net profit) / (1 − t), divided by pocket revenue R.
